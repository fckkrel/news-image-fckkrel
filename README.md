One image summing up the world's news of [Friday 20 November 2026](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120), for make.fun's "Sum up 20 November's world news in one image" bounty.

The front page carries 10 stories across 7 regions: Russia's strikes on Kyiv's Dnipro bridges and missiles landing in Poland; the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin three-way summit in Shenzhen; the World Court's genocide ruling against Myanmar; the global AI sell-off; Cyclone Akvan's landfall in Odisha; the UK–EU deals; COP31 in Antalya; the US Senate's Iran war-powers vote; and the 6.7 earthquake in central Peru — plus the day's market closes.

`entry.png` is rendered from `entry.html` (a newspaper front page, 1024×1024) and `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png`. No network, no dependencies, Node 22 standard library only.
